1. Who is responsible

Nexora AI is operated by kimjiho.dev, based in Nigeria. A public street address has not been included in this draft; confirm whether one is legally required before publication. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact verify.taskflow@gmail.com.

2. What this site does today

The Nexora AI Supabase project is configured for authentication, but sign-up is blocked while the required policies are drafts. The site currently has no analytics or advertising scripts in its source.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. Those providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser downloads those resources. Confirm the providers’ current terms and notices before launch.

3. Account information when sign-up is enabled

The planned account flow sends the name and email address you enter, and your password over HTTPS, to the Supabase project configured by Nexora AI. Supabase Auth manages password credentials; the Nexora AI pages do not intentionally store a plaintext password. Supabase may process technical and security data such as IP address and authentication events. Confirm the actual data, project region, retention, and processor terms in the deployed configuration.

If Google sign-in is enabled, Google will process sign-in information under Google’s own privacy terms. Email verification and password-reset messages will use the email-delivery provider configured for the Supabase project.

4. Session storage

The Supabase browser client is configured to persist an authentication session in browser local storage so you can stay signed in. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access that session. Sign out on shared devices and protect your device.

5. Why information is used

Planned uses include creating and securing accounts, verifying email addresses, signing users in, sending requested account-recovery messages, responding to support requests, and preventing abuse. The applicable legal basis for each purpose must be determined for the jurisdictions where Nexora AI operates.

6. Service providers and sharing

Account information will be shared with Supabase to provide authentication. Google may be involved if you choose Google sign-in. Website hosting, email delivery, fonts, and the JavaScript CDN may also process technical data. Before publication, list each active provider, purpose, data category, and relevant transfer safeguards. Nexora AI should not claim a provider is used or data is never shared without confirming the production setup.

7. Retention and deletion

Our proposed practice is to retain account information while an account is active. You may request account closure or deletion at verify.taskflow@gmail.com. After verifying a request, we aim to remove the account from active systems within 30 days, except for information we must retain for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. Copies in backups may remain for up to 90 days before routine expiry. These timeframes are proposals and must be checked against Supabase’s actual backup, log, and deletion behavior before this notice is approved. Support correspondence will be retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; the exact schedule requires confirmation.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain uses. Contact verify.taskflow@gmail.com to make a request or raise a concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Confirm the applicable rights, response periods, and regulator or complaint route under Nigerian law before publication.

9. Children and security

Nexora AI is intended for people aged 16 or older and is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal information, contact us at verify.taskflow@gmail.com so we can review the request. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can promise absolute security; confirm the safeguards and incident-response process before publication.

10. International transfers and changes

Nexora AI uses providers that may process information in countries outside Nigeria. Before publication, confirm the locations, providers, and any safeguards required by applicable law.
