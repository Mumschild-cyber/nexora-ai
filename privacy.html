2. What this site does today

The Yola Supabase project is configured for authentication, but sign-up is blocked while the required policies are drafts. The site currently has no analytics or advertising scripts in its source.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. Those providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser downloads those resources. Confirm the providers’ current terms and notices before launch.

3. Account information when sign-up is enabled

The planned account flow sends the name and email address you enter, and your password over HTTPS, to the Supabase project configured by Yola. Supabase Auth manages password credentials; the Yola pages do not intentionally store a plaintext password. Supabase may process technical and security data such as IP address and authentication events. Confirm the actual data, project region, retention, and processor terms in the deployed configuration.

If Google sign-in is enabled, Google will process sign-in information under Google’s own privacy terms. Email verification and password-reset messages will use the email-delivery provider configured for the Supabase project.

4. Session storage

The Supabase browser client is configured to persist an authentication session in browser local storage so you can stay signed in. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access that session. Sign out on shared devices and protect your device.

5. Why information is used

Planned uses include creating and securing accounts, verifying email addresses, signing users in, sending requested account-recovery messages, responding to support requests, and preventing abuse. The applicable legal basis for each purpose must be determined for the jurisdictions where Yola operates.

6. Service providers and sharing

Account information will be shared with Supabase to provide authentication. Google may be involved if you choose Google sign-in. Website hosting, email delivery, fonts, and the JavaScript CDN may also process technical data. Before publication, list each active provider, purpose, data category, and relevant transfer safeguards. Yola should not claim a provider is used or data is never shared without confirming the production setup.
