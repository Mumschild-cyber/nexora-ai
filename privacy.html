Your information matters

Privacy, with care.

This notice explains how Nexora AI handles information when you use the website and account features.

1. Who is responsible

Nexora AI is operated by kimjiho.dev, based in Nigeria. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact verify.taskflow@gmail.com.

2. What this site does today

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses Supabase for account authentication, Google Fonts to display text, and jsDelivr to load the Supabase JavaScript library. The website does not currently include analytics or advertising scripts.

When your browser requests these services, their providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested resource. Each provider handles that information under its own privacy notice.

3. Account information

When you create an account, the information you enter, including your name, email address, and password, is sent over HTTPS to Supabase Auth. Supabase manages password credentials; Nexora AI pages do not intentionally store your password in plaintext. Supabase may also process technical and security information such as IP addresses and authentication events. The Supabase project is configured in the Europe region.

Email verification and password-reset messages are handled through the email service configured for Supabase Auth. Google sign-in is not currently enabled.

4. Session storage

The Supabase browser client is configured to persist an authentication session in browser local storage so you can stay signed in. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access that session. Sign out on shared devices and protect your device.

5. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, sign users in, send requested account-recovery messages, respond to support and privacy requests, and help prevent abuse.

6. Service providers and sharing

Supabase processes account information to provide authentication. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google Fonts and jsDelivr process requests needed to load fonts and the Supabase client library. These providers may process technical information as described above. Google is not currently used for sign-in. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while your account is active. You may request account closure or deletion at verify.taskflow@gmail.com. After verifying your request, we aim to remove account information from active systems within 30 days, except for information that must be retained for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. Copies in backups may remain for up to 90 days before routine expiry. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain uses. Contact verify.taskflow@gmail.com to make a request or raise a concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also contact the relevant data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

9. Children and security

Nexora AI is intended for people aged 16 or older and is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal information, contact us at verify.taskflow@gmail.com so we can review the request. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers and changes

Our service providers may process information in countries outside Nigeria, including in the region where the Supabase project is hosted. We will update this notice when our information practices materially change.
